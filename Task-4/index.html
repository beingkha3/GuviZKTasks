How to Play Memory Game

  • Click on a card to flip it and reveal the symbol.
  • Click on a second card to try to find a match.
  • If the cards match, they stay flipped. If not, they flip back after a moment.
  • Find all matching pairs to win!
  • Try to complete the game in as few moves and as little time as possible.
